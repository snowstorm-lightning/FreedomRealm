# Code of Conduct

FreedomRealm prioritizes individual contributors, open-source maintainers, small teams, community organizations, and voluntary collaboration.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and specific.
- Assume contributions are voluntary; do not pressure people through AI assignment, rankings, reminders, or contribution scores.
- Credit code, docs, templates, eval samples, failure reports, review notes, translations, design discussions, and usage reports.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when commercial or organizational incentives may affect a discussion.
- Protect private data, sensitive data, and contributor safety.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Impersonating maintainers, official releases, compatibility certification, or project identity.
- Publishing private communications, credentials, production data, or sensitive raw user data.
- Using AI-generated assignments or contribution analytics to coerce contributors.
- Attempting to bypass `ApprovalGate`, audit, security, budget, data classification, or human review.

Reporting:

- For security-sensitive issues, use `SECURITY.md`.
- For conduct or governance concerns, open a GitHub issue only if it is safe to discuss publicly.
- If public discussion would expose private data or harm a contributor, contact a maintainer through a private channel first.

Maintainers should handle reports with evidence-first review, minimal necessary disclosure, conflict-of-interest checks, and an appeal path.
