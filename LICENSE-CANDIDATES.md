# License Candidates

FreedomRealm has not selected a final project license yet. This file is decision material, not legal advice and not a license grant.

The current recommended direction is:

- `AGPL-3.0` for stronger network-service reciprocity.
- Trademark and official compatibility rules to protect the FreedomRealm identity.
- Open protocol, template, evaluation, and Commons governance so community value remains auditable and reusable.

Other candidates under review:

| Candidate | Why consider it | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AGPL-3.0` | Better fit for network services and anti-closed-SaaS capture goals | More sensitive for some commercial adopters |
| `Apache-2.0` | Enterprise-friendly, includes patent terms | Weaker against closed hosted forks |
| `MIT` | Simple and familiar | Minimal protection against closed capture |
| `MPL-2.0` | File-level copyleft with integration flexibility | Weaker network-service coverage than AGPL |

Until a maintainer/community decision is recorded:

- Do not treat this repository as carrying a final OSI license.
- Do not copy project branding, logo, official compatibility claims, or release identity as if they were granted.
- Contributions are accepted for review under the expectation that the final license may be one of the candidates above.
- If this uncertainty is a blocker, open a governance discussion before contributing substantial code.
